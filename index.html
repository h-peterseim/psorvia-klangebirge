PSOrvia Klangebirge

Audio-reactive mountain range for your website

A calm landscape as page background. The spectrum shapes the mountains: bass becomes broad massifs, treble becomes narrow peaks. You fly through a valley; the music raises the mountains around you as they come towards you.

PSOrvia · psorvia.io

Press the button: the music shapes the mountains. Your own audio or video works the same way. “Still image” shows what visitors with reduced motion see.

Usage

<script src="klangebirge.js"></script>
<klang-gebirge></klang-gebirge>
